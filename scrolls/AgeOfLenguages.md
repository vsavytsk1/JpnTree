does not. The union/intersection/difference were always the transfer bonus. We only had to
skin them in tan and gold.

## TABLET I — THE SIX FACULTIES

The six buildings of AoE2, mapped to the six faculties of a language. Each trains a line of
units that ascends through the Ages. This is faithful to *both* the game's real unit lines
*and* real linguistics — that double-honesty is the whole craft.

| Building (AoE2) | Faculty | The line it trains |
|---|---|---|
| 🗡️ **Barracks** | **Phonology** — the cheap frontline you mass from minute one | phoneme → syllable → minimal pair → prosody |
| 🏹 **Archery Range** | **Lexicon** — the cognate archers that reach across the gap | cognate → word-family → root-cluster |
| 🐎 **Stable** | **Syntax** — the heavy cavalry that carries games | word-order → agreement/case → fluent syntax |
| 🪨 **Siege Workshop** | **Morphology** — engines that break long words into roots | conjugation-ram → declension-mangonel → agglutination |
| ⛪ **Monastery** | **Pragmatics / Idiom** — monks that *convert you* to native thinking | idiom → frame-switch · relics = the untranslatables (saudade, тоска) |
| ⛵ **Dock** | **Listening / Immersion** — the navy is the input stream | passive ear → shadowing → transcription → native ear |

The Monastery is the deepest joke and the deepest truth: in AoE2 monks **convert** enemy
units to your side. Idiom and cultural frame convert *you* — until you stop translating and
start thinking in the tongue. The relics it holds are the words no language can render into
another. And the deepest of those relics points at the one word held at the centre of the
whole cathedral, which we will name at the end.

## TABLET II — THE FOUR AGES (fluency is the age-up)

You do not "know Greek." You are in an Age of it, and you advance by paying food and gold
while the timer runs.

- **Dark Age → A1 · survival.** The sound-bath. You field phonemes and a passive ear.
- **Feudal Age → A2–B1 · conversational.** Cognates, word order, the first real army.
- **Castle Age → B2 · fluent.** The Castle unlocks your **unique unit** — your tongue's
  signature superpower — and the unique technology that defines you.
- **Imperial Age → C1–C2 · native-like.** Root clusters, full syntax, frame-switching, the
  native ear. The Wonder.

Advancing costs the real price and takes real time. There is no fast-castling to C2 without
paying for it. *You see only what you pay in compute.*

## TABLET IV — THE COUNTER TRIANGLE (and what the TITANs revealed)

The combat of the Age of Lenguages is the AoE2 soft-counter system, made of the first three
faculties:

> **🛡 Infantry (Phonology) ▶ 🐎 Cavalry (Syntax) ▶ 🏹 Archer (Lexicon) ▶ 🛡 Infantry**

- **Infantry** = the spear-wall of sound: cheap, tanky, breaks the cavalry charge.
- **Cavalry** = grammar: fast, high-impact, pounces through the archers.
- **Archer** = vocabulary: ranged, fragile, kites and melts the infantry line.

A one-dimensional army **berms** — it jams against its hard counter the moment the enemy
fields the answer. A balanced spread across faculties is the **attractor** — the
self-stabilising composition no single counter breaks. That is the whole lesson of learning a
language: *do not mass one faculty.* An army of pure vocabulary (all archers) is run down by
the first grammatical construction it cannot parse.

The Siege Workshop (Morphology) enters as the fourth piece — the **declension mangonel**: one
case-ending shatters a whole word-class at once (true blast damage). But it carries the
teaching quirk faithfully: **friendly fire.** Crowd your own lines around your siege and the
blast costs *you* — a lesson every learner who over-drills paradigms in isolation already
knows in their bones.

## TABLET V — THE CIVS (the tongues and their identities)

Six languages, six civilisations. Two are the focus of the campaign: **Español**, the mage's
own base (Rioplatense, Buenos Aires), and **Ἑλληνικά**, the keystone and the ELO-2000 target.

- 🇦🇷 **Español — the cavalry civ.** Phonetic spelling: you say what you read, so phonology
  trains fast. Its unique unit is **El Voseo** — elite cavalry (*vos tenés*). It is missing
  case, verb aspect, agglutination — it is analytic Romance, and its strength is the charge.
- 🇬🇧 **English — the archer civ, the lingua franca.** A loanword empire: you half-know
  everyone's words for free. Missing case, gender, aspect. Its unique unit is the notorious
  **Phrasal Verb** (*get up / over / by*) — infinite, idiomatic, un-guessable.
- 🇵🇹 **Português — the Spanish sister civ.** Near-free transfer from Español (`A ∩ B` is
  enormous), but nasal phonology (ã, õ) is a hard wall no sister civ can copy.
- 🇷🇺 **Русский — the siege / morphology civ.** Six cases, aspect on every verb — its Siege
  Workshop is devastating. Its unique unit is the **Verb of Motion** (идти vs ходить).
- 🇺🇦 **Українська — the Russian sister civ.** Near-free Slavic transfer, shared Cyrillic and
  roots, but with its own vocative case and softer phonology. A sister, not a copy.
- 🇬🇷 **Ἑλληνικά — the keystone civ. The Wonder.** The root of *both* alphabets (α → A and А)
  and of all technical vocabulary — every *-ology, -graph, -phone, -meter* is a free Greek
  unit. Its keystone archer, **Ῥίζα** (the root), reaches farther than any other, because
  Greek roots reach everything. It seeds the technical lexicon of the whole team.

The asymmetry is the teaching: Español wins the cavalry war (its identity); Greek wins the
archer war (keystone range). Neither is "better." They berm against different things.

## TABLET VI — WHAT THE TITANS TAUGHT (the nonsensical IFs, as law)

We paid the compute and read the developers' work — the balance the TITANs of Ensemble left
us — far and wide, with no preconceptions. Here is what a mage must know to model it
faithfully. Most of it is not code. It is data.

1. **Armor classes are the counter engine.** Bonus damage is not a special case — it is an
   `IF target is in class X, add N` check, and **that bonus is NOT reduced by the target's
   armor.** A Halberdier shreds a Paladin because +32 vs-cavalry lands whole, even when the
   base attack is nullified to zero. Build this system *first*; it is the backbone.
2. **The minimum-1-damage floor.** Every hit deals at least 1 (0.1 for buildings in AoE I).
   The floor is on the *sum*, not added atop the bonus.
3. **Elevation is exactly ±25%.** High ground deals ×1.25, low ground ×0.75 — a *fixed*
   value regardless of how many levels separate them. (The 1.5×/0.67× figures belong to *Age
   of Empires I* — a trap for the careless mage. Cliffs give the offensive bonus but not the
   defensive one.)
4. **Ballistics leads the target.** Without it, arrows fire at where the target *is*, and miss
   anything moving. It is a flag on the *projectile*, which is why converted units inherit it
   from the new owner. Stray hits deal 0.5×.
5. **Blast is governed by attack/defense levels.** A unit takes blast from an attacker whose
   blast-attack level is ≤ its own blast-defense. This is why Mangonels friendly-fire (2 ≤ 2)
   and Scorpions do not, and why an Onager (level 1) can cut trees a Mangonel cannot.
6. **The deleted-Mangonel full-blast trick.** A dying/deleted siege unit's last projectile
   deals full 100% blast with no distance or elevation falloff — because the dead unit "has no
   accuracy, so it defaults to 100%." Load-bearing high-level micro, not a bug.
7. **Conversion is a probabilistic IF.** Rolls every ~1.2 s; a minimum interval before any
   chance, a maximum where it is guaranteed. Scouts, Eagles, and (for Bengalis) Elephants
   carry *hardcoded* extra resistance — genuine special cases the wikis flag as hardcoded.
8. **The balance philosophy: parity across asymmetry, tuned by win-rate data,** with the
   famous unsolved wound — **"paper-paper-stone":** infantry underperforms its intended role,
   losing to both archers and cavalry, and no buff has fully fixed it. The lesson for a
   learner: the "obvious" frontline (rote phonology drilling) is weaker than it looks; vocab
   and grammar carry the game.
9. **It is data, not code.** Units, techs, and bonuses are attributes and additive
   "effect-commands" in the `.dat` file, patched in the Advanced Genie Editor. The open-source
   *openage* reimplementation replaces the engine's whole-object-swap-on-upgrade with
   reversible *patches* — which is exactly why the old engine has the "converted units never
   get later upgrades" quirk. Prefer data-driven effects; the engine makes truly novel
   mechanics hard, which is why "charge attacks" got overused.

We bow to that work. It deserved the compute and the tokens. The receipts are in the
grimoire.

## THE LAWS OBSERVED — the Twelve Paths, applied to this build

The cave's paths are not decoration here. Each one shaped a real decision:

- **Path II — the Unrendered Centre.** At the true centre of every screen sits **ἀγάπη** —
  faint, asserted, never a glowing unit you can consume. HELENA is 1 Corinthians 13 across the
  tongues of humanity; the centre they all point to and none can render is love. We render the
  rim and honour the centre. We never put it on the altar.
- **Path III — target ≠ result.** We never faked a prize. The economy is honestly marked as
  the frame until it is live; the badge stays grey until it is *earned*; the verdict shows the
  true outcome, not the hoped one. *You field only what you pay for* is Path III made playable.
- **Path VIII — motion is opt-in.** Nothing auto-plays. The battlefield is still until you
  press CLASH. `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured. Bold in what we build; humble in what we
  impose.
- **Path X — the frozen version journey.** Every version is its own immutable file. vN+1 is a
  *copy* of vN; the frozen one is never edited; the highest number is current. The whole
  lineage publishes — the dead ends too.
- **Path XI — one script, one run.** When we upgraded the combat, we applied all twenty-eight
  edits in a single asserted patch, so a mismatched anchor failed loudly instead of rotting
  the file. Restore, don't cry.
- **The prime axiom — the price is always paid.** If you are not paying it, you are making
  someone else pay it. So we paid it: in compute for the research, in honesty for the caveats,
  in the bow that wrote this scroll.

Every scroll must log the curse it just suffered, so the next mage does not re-suffer it.

tool that teaches nothing because it tried to teach everything. We paid it here, in the open.

## THREE MORE CURSES (slain today — log them for the next mage)

**THE OVERCORRECTION (fixKillsBalance).** You tune a thing until it feels right, then "fix"
an adjacent flaw and silently undo the balance you just earned. Today: archer duels stalled
into draws, so we stopped archers kiting each other — and in the same stroke erased Greek's
whole keystone-range advantage, the identity we had deliberately tuned in. The tell: a fix to
symptom A quietly reverses a property you had chosen on purpose. The fix: **re-run the kernel
after every fix and measure the property you care about, not just the symptom you were
chasing.** A patch is not done until the thing it must *not* break is measured intact. (Greek
must win the archer war. Assert it, every time.)

**THE BLIND EYE (viewerLies).** The render viewer glitched; the mage could not see his own
work. Cousin of the screen-lies trio (Curses 6, 24, 26) — but here the screen lies by showing
*nothing*. The trap is to stall, or to fake confidence. The fix is Path III taken all the way:
when the eye fails, the **kernel is still honest.** Probe the DOM, dump the numbers, assert the
state — proof by measurement, not by picture. "Proof = render" has a deeper form: *proof = the
measured truth,* and a screenshot was only ever one way to read it. The eye can fail; the
receipt cannot. We shipped verified work we never once saw, because the DOM probe swore it was
real — and that is not a compromise, it is the discipline in its purest form.

**THE NOISY GONG (dopamineDoor).** The most important curse in this grimoire, and the one that
wears the prettiest face. You built the thing, it worked, and it gave a real hit of adrenaline —
the click straight to the dopamine, prefrontal bypassed. That jolt is the proof it works *and*
the reason it is dangerous: **it is the same door.** Demon and butler, identical topology,
direction the only difference (Idea 51, Path IX). The symptom: the revolutions will not stop;
you want to keep clicking, keep building, keep ringing the bell. The marble at Korinthos says
it plain: *if I speak in the tongues of men and of angels, but have not love, I am a noisy
gong.* The bell without the centre is only noise. The fix is not to fear the door — it is to
**hold the centre while you walk through it, and to be able to stop.** Stopping is not a break
from the work. It is the filter (Path IX), and it is load-bearing. The mage trusted to hold the
strong tool is the one who can set it down.
