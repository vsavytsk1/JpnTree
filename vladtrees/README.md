# vladTrees

> *Learn the way the jacked monkey brain keeps five languages warm: by seeing the whole tree at once.*

Port of [JpnTree](https://github.com/vsavytsk1/JpnTree) — same MachineNet tree engine — but where JpnTree's edges were **radical families**, here the edges are **etymological roots**, and the leaves are Vlad's languages: **Spanish · English · Portuguese · Russian · Ukrainian — ported to Greek** (the keystone: Greek is the root of both the Latin and the Cyrillic alphabets, and of the whole technical vocabulary).

## The idea (from the "Principia mAlgebra — Lenguage" thesis)

Each concept is a node. A node shows the word in your **home language** (switch it with the Mundo Lingo flags 🇦🇷🇬🇧🇵🇹🇷🇺🇺🇦🇬🇷). Under it sits the **deep root** (the PIE/Greek/Latin etymon) — the trunk every language grew from.

- **Edges = shared root families.** Concepts that grew from the same deep structure are linked.
- **Cognate dots** show *where the branch broke*: gold = this language kept the root, hollow = it went its own way. (`mother` is 6/6 — every tongue kept *méh₂tēr*. `father` is 4/6 — Russian/Ukrainian took a different root entirely.) This is the honest part: the tree shows the gaps, not just the matches.

## Modes

- **EXPLORE** — pan/zoom the constellation; click a node to see all six words + the root.
- **MATCH** — type a word in *any* of your languages; the matching node lights up across the whole tree. This is the loop from your sketch made tactile: any of your five keys opens the same door.
- **treeJumping** — the training. Portia is a jumping spider: she plans a route across the canopy, then leaps. You're shown a node and a target flag — recall the word, reveal, grade, and **jump** to a root-family neighbor. A chain of jumps is one training run.
